## Writing for Spain first; Latin America only with care

- **Money:** EUR · **numbers:** 1.234,56 € · **VAT:** 21% (10%, 4%)
- **Tax words:** IVA; NIF
- **Watch out:** Spain: Verifactu and B2B e-invoicing are coming (check dates). Mexico (CFDI) and most of Latin America require electronic invoices: don't present a PDF as a valid invoice there.
- **Search words** usually look like: template "plantilla factura {trade}", app page "programa de facturación para {trade}", tool "calculadora de {tool}". Research the real local words first (the demand gate): Google in Spanish (autocomplete, "People also ask"), Search Console for the es pages.
- **Every page:** written for the country, not word for word; slug, title, H1 and examples in
  Spanish; links to the English page (hreflang) and to the other es pages; the same depth and
  honesty rules as the English page ([PLAN.md](../../PLAN.md#rules-for-every-page)).
- **Skip a page** that has no Spanish search or doesn't fit the country: a `parked` row in
  `es` with `translation_of` = the English URL and the reason in `notes`.
