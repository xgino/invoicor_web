## Writing for Poland

- **Money:** PLN · **numbers:** 1 234,56 zł · **VAT:** 23% (8%, 5%)
- **Tax words:** VAT; NIP
- **Watch out:** Poland: KSeF structured e-invoices become mandatory in 2026 (check dates); after that a PDF isn't a valid B2B invoice.
- **Search words** usually look like: template "wzór faktury {trade}", app page "program do faktur dla {trade}", tool "kalkulator {tool}". Research the real local words first (the demand gate): Google in Polish (autocomplete, "People also ask"), Search Console for the pl pages.
- **Every page:** written for the country, not word for word; slug, title, H1 and examples in
  Polish; links to the English page (hreflang) and to the other pl pages; the same depth and
  honesty rules as the English page ([PLAN.md](../../PLAN.md#rules-for-every-page)).
- **Skip a page** that has no Polish search or doesn't fit the country: a `parked` row in
  `pl` with `translation_of` = the English URL and the reason in `notes`.
