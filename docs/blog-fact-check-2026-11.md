# Blog fact check: learn.invoicor.com

**Date:** 2026-11-18
**Checked against:** `BRAND.md` section 10 (fact sheet). Nothing else counts as proof.
**Articles read:** 17 (all of them, from `sitemap-1.xml`)
**Problems found:** 129 (one row per spot to fix; most are the same five lines repeated)

All 17 articles come from the same AI template. They repeat the same few claims, so most fixes are the
same sentence swapped in many places. Fix the five "stock lines" below once, then search and replace
across all articles. The per-article tables list every spot.

## The five stock lines (use these everywhere)

| Code | Says now | Replace with |
|---|---|---|
| **S5** | "create a professional invoice in under 5 seconds" | "Make an invoice in seconds once your clients and products are saved." |
| **S80** | "80% auto fill" / "auto-fill most of the details" / "Invoicor says it can auto fill 80%" | "Saved clients and products fill in for you, and you can duplicate a past invoice in one tap." |
| **TAX** | "built-in tax calculator" | "Invoicor works out the tax and the total for you. You set one tax rate per invoice, added on top or included in the price." |
| **FREE** | "free to start" / "Is it free to start? Yes." | "Your first 3 invoices are free, with no subscription and no watermark. After that, Starter or Pro." |
| **CUR** | "multi-currency support" (no number) | "Pick the currency for each invoice from 38 currencies. The invoice itself can be in one of 11 languages." |

Why: "under 5 seconds" and "80%" are numbers nobody has measured (BRAND.md §5 says keep the 5-second claim
provable). "Tax calculator" makes people expect per-line tax or rate lookup, which the app doesn't do.
"Free to start" hides the 3-invoice limit (BRAND.md §6 rule 5: never "free" without the limit nearby).

## Links (all articles)

- Every article links to invoicor.com only through the header and footer ("Home", `https://invoicor.com/`).
  No article links to a specific invoicor.com page inside the text.
- The "Get Paid Faster" button goes to the App Store. That's fine, but "Get Paid Faster" is a promise we
  can't prove. Suggest **"Get the app (3 invoices free)"**. It sits in the blog's shared template, so it's one fix.
- Suggested link per article is listed below. Put it in the first or second paragraph.
